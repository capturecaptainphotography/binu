How the games room works

Two ways to play, every game 🎯

Pick a game from the shelf, then choose your players. You can switch between the two modes in the middle of a game.

👥

2 Players

Two people share one screen. Take turns on the same device — perfect for brothers, cousins and best friends.

🤖

vs Computer

Playing on your own? The system plays against you. Chess plans a few moves ahead, checkers looks even deeper.

🔊

Sound & motion

Pieces pop, dice tumble, cards flip and the winner gets a fanfare. Tap the speaker in the corner to mute it.
