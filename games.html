Welcome to our games room! 🎮

Binu and Himu built this corner of the website together. There are 26 games inside, and every one has 5 levels (memory match has 7) — that is 132 levels to beat! Start at level 1, win to unlock the next one. Chess, ludo, checkers, snakes & ladders, pong, memory, and four big action games: running 🏃, shooting 🚀, boxing 🥊 and car racing 🏎️. Play two players on one screen, play online with a friend using a game ID, or take on the computer.

How the games room works

Two ways to play, five levels each, one trophy shelf 🎯

Pick a game from the shelf, choose your players, then climb the levels. Switch between the two modes in the middle of a game.

🎚️

5 levels every game

Level 1 is friendly, level 5 is expert. Bigger boards, more cards, faster balls and a cleverer computer each time. Win to unlock the next level.

👤

Sign in — no email needed

Just pick a name and a secret password. Your levels, stars and trophies are kept for you, and your brother keeps his own.

🏆

Trophies & stars

Every game you play earns stars and trophies. The trophy shelf remembers your best level in all 26 games.

🤖

vs Computer

Playing on your own? The system plays against you. Chess plans a few moves ahead, checkers looks even deeper.

🌐

Play online with a game ID

Far apart? One of you taps Play online and makes a 4-letter game ID, the other types that ID in on their own device — then take turns on the same board from two houses.

🎨

Your names, your colours

Open the players panel and give everyone a name, a face and a colour. Every game says the real names, and the boards are painted in your colours.

🔊

Sound & motion

Pieces pop, dice tumble, cards flip and the winner gets a fanfare. Tap the speaker in the corner to mute it.
